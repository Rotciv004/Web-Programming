Mercedes-Benz A-Class

The A-Class is a subcompact luxury car that serves as the entry-level model for Mercedes-Benz. Available as a hatchback or sedan (in some markets), it offers a youthful design, modern technology, and a premium interior in a compact package. Despite its smaller size, the A-Class features the advanced MBUX infotainment system and many safety and driver-assistance technologies found in larger Mercedes models.

Engine 2.0L Turbo I4
Horsepower 188 hp (approx)
Base Price $35,000 (estimated)
  • Subcompact luxury hatchback with sporty styling
  • MBUX infotainment system with voice control
  • Advanced driver assistance features available
  • Efficient turbocharged engine options

Mercedes-Benz S-Class

The S-Class is the flagship luxury sedan of Mercedes-Benz, renowned for its cutting-edge technology, comfort, and performance. It has a long history of introducing innovative features and setting the benchmark for luxury vehicles worldwide. The latest S-Class offers a spacious, opulent interior with extensive use of fine leather, wood, and ambient lighting, along with advanced features like a digital dashboard, sophisticated infotainment, and semi-autonomous driving capabilities.

Engine 3.0L Turbo I6 (mild hybrid)
Horsepower 429 hp (approx)
Base Price $110,000 (estimated)
  • Full-size flagship sedan with cutting-edge technology
  • Ultra-luxurious interior with premium materials and comfort features
  • Advanced driver assistance (semi-autonomous driving capabilities)
  • Pioneers new features (e.g., digital lights, augmented reality displays)

Mercedes-Benz G-Class

The G-Class (or G-Wagon) is an iconic luxury SUV known for its rugged off-road capability and classic, boxy design. Originally developed as a military off-roader in the late 1970s, the G-Class has evolved into a status symbol that blends old-school toughness with modern luxury. It features a body-on-frame construction and permanent all-wheel drive with three locking differentials, allowing it to tackle difficult terrain. At the same time, the latest G-Class offers a refined interior with leather seats and the latest infotainment features, making it both adventurous and comfortable.

Engine 4.0L Biturbo V8
Horsepower 416 hp (G 550)
Base Price $140,000 (estimated)
  • Iconic boxy design with body-on-frame construction
  • Three locking differentials for extreme off-road capability
  • Powerful V8 engine with full-time 4×4 drivetrain
  • Modern luxurious interior with advanced tech features

Mercedes-AMG GT

The Mercedes-AMG GT is a high-performance sports car developed by the Mercedes-AMG division. Available as a two-door coupe (and roadster in some variants), it features a front-mid engine layout and rear-wheel drive, delivering an exhilarating driving experience. The AMG GT is powered by a hand-built 4.0L twin-turbo V8 engine in various states of tune (producing well over 500 horsepower in top versions). Its sleek design, low profile, and aggressive styling underline its track-focused nature, while still providing the luxury and refinement expected from Mercedes-Benz.

Engine 4.0L Biturbo V8
Horsepower 523 hp (approx)
Base Price $115,000 (estimated)
  • Two-seat sports coupe with lightweight aluminum construction
  • Handcrafted AMG V8 engine with immense power and torque
  • 7-speed dual-clutch transmission for rapid gear changes
  • Performance-tuned suspension and aerodynamics for track use

Did You Know?

  • The name Mercedes comes from Mercédès Jellinek, the daughter of an early 20th-century Daimler distributor; the brand Mercedes-Benz was created in 1926 when Karl Benz’s company merged with Daimler.
  • The three-pointed star logo represents Mercedes-Benz’s ambition to motorize transportation on land, water, and air.
  • Mercedes-Benz has pioneered many safety innovations in automobiles, including the crumple zone (1950s), anti-lock braking system (ABS, introduced in 1978), and the airbag (introduced in 1981).
